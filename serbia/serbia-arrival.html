Chapter I: The Arrival

LOG ENTRY // LOCATION: BELGRADE, SERBIA // STATUS: CONNECTED

Airports are the same everywhere. Neon lights, overpriced coffee, and people running away from themselves. I just landed in Belgrade. No plans, no hotel, just a backpack and this heavy emptiness I carry around.

At passport control, the officer looked at my Italian passport, then at my face. "Business or tourism?" he asked in broken English. I gave him my first Serbian code:

"Ja sam turista." // (I am a tourist)

He stamped it. No questions asked.

I walked straight past the baggage claim. Luggage is for people who are afraid to leave their past behind. All I have is my laptop, my phone, and the clothes on my back.

Before facing the city, I needed to hack into its network. I found a Yettel kiosk.

"Želim internet." // (I want internet)

A quick tap of my card, and for a few euros, I was connected.

I ignored the fake taxi drivers shouting at the exit with a cold:

"Ne treba, hvala." // (No need, thanks)

I opened the Yandex Go app, got a car, looked at the driver in the rearview mirror, and gave him the final command for the night:

"Idem u centar." // (I am going to the center)

He just nodded, lit a cigarette, and drove into the gray mist. The madness begins.
